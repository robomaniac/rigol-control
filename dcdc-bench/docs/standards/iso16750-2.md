# ISO 16750-2:2023, section 4 (electrical loads): clause map and bench verdicts

This page lists every clause of section 4 of ISO 16750-2:2023 with its test
parameters for 12 V and 24 V systems, the instrument capability each clause
needs, and what that means on the project's bench (Rigol DP821A CH1 as the
source, 0-35.8 V / 1 A in the purpose-limited profile, commanded over LAN at
about 1 s; Rigol DL3031A as the load). The machine-readable form is
`src/dcdc_bench/standards.py`; the bench page's "Which test?" checklist reads
that module, not this page.

Only technical facts (clause numbers, titles, levels, durations, rates,
impedances, functional status classes) are recorded here, cited as
"ISO 16750-2:2023, clause 4.x". The standard's text is not reproduced and the
reference copy is not stored in the repository (implementation brief §16).
Values that appear only inside a figure of the standard are marked
**figure value**; confirm them on the printed copy before relying on them.

## Definitions used below

- **UN** nominal system voltage: 12 V or 24 V. **UA** supply voltage with the
  alternator running: 14 V / 28 V. **UB** with the alternator stopped:
  12 V / 24 V. (ISO 16750-1.)
- **Usmin / Usmax** minimum and maximum supply voltage of the chosen code
  (ISO 16750-2:2023, clause 4.2, Tables 3 and 4):

  | System | Code | Usmin | Usmax |
  | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
  | 12 V | A | 6 V | 16 V |
  | 12 V | B | 8 V | 16 V |
  | 12 V | C | 9 V | 16 V |
  | 12 V | D | 10.5 V | 16 V |
  | 24 V | E | 10 V | 32 V |
  | 24 V | F | 16 V | 32 V |
  | 24 V | H | 18 V | 32 V |
  | 24 V | G | 22 V | 32 V |
  | both | Z | as agreed | as agreed |

  For the 12T12-4A (stated 9-36 V input) the module selects the code whose
  range fits inside the DUT rating with the lowest Usmin: **C** for 12 V,
  **E** for 24 V.
- **Operating modes** (ISO 16750-1): 1.x not energised; 2.x energised, functions
  not activated (2.1 sleep); 3.2 normal operating load; 3.3 minimum load;
  3.4 maximum load.
- **Functional status classes** (ISO 16750-1), one line each:
  **A** every function works as designed during and after the test;
  **B** every function works during the test, some may leave tolerance, all
  recover by themselves afterwards;
  **C** some functions stop during the test and come back by themselves
  afterwards;
  **D** some functions stop during the test and need a simple operator reset;
  **E** some functions stop and do not come back without repair or replacement.
- **General tolerances** (clause 4.1): frequency and time +/-5 %, voltage
  +/-0.2 V, current +/-2 %, inductance +/-10 %, resistance +/-10 %. Voltages
  are measured at the DUT terminals; at least two DUTs are used.

Where 12 V and 24 V differ, the standard differs: 4.2 codes, 4.3.1.1 level,
4.3.1.2 applicability, 4.3.2 edges and level, 4.4 severity-1 ripple, 4.5
start level, 4.6.1.1 drop level, 4.6.3 every timing, 4.6.4 levels and
impedances, 4.7 test cases, 4.8 US. The rate of 4.5, the step logic of 4.6.2
and the timings of 4.6.1.2, 4.9 and 4.10 are the same for both systems.

## Numbering versus the owner's recollection

The owner remembered 4.6.2 for reset behaviour, 4.6.4 for load dump and 4.7
for reversed voltage. All three match the 2023 edition. The 2023 edition adds
sub-clauses rather than renumbering: 4.3 now has 4.3.1.1 (alternator failure,
formerly the whole of 4.3), 4.3.1.2 (jump start, new) and 4.3.2 (transient
overvoltage, new); 4.6.1 now has 4.6.1.1 (momentary drop, formerly the whole of
4.6.1) and 4.6.1.2 (micro interruption, new). Pulses 4 and 5 of older
ISO 7637-2 editions are the starting profile (4.6.3) and load dump (4.6.4) here.

## Capability tokens and what the bench earns

| Token | Meaning | DP821A CH1 profile (real or mock) |
| --- | --- | :-: |
| `dc_steady` | DC level held >= 30 s | yes |
| `dc_step_1s` | DC steps with >= 1 s holds at the LAN cadence; the edge is the supply's own, uncharacterised slew | yes |
| `dc_ramp_slow` | ramp <= about 1 V/s as bounded live steps (<= 1 V per >= 1 s step) | yes |
| `edge_10ms` | rise/fall guaranteed within 10 ms | no |
| `negative_voltage` | output below 0 V (the contract forbids a negative minimum) | never |
| `ac_superposition` | ripple 10 Hz-200 kHz on the DC level | no |
| `pulse_ms` / `pulse_us` | pulses and edges defined in ms / us | no |
| `low_source_impedance_pulse` | generator with 0.5-8 Ohm source impedance | no |
| `line_switch_10Mohm` | series switch opening to >= 10 MOhm | no |
| `floating_offset_source`, `short_circuit_fixture`, `hipot_tester`, `insulation_tester` | see module docstring | no |
| `iso7637_transient_generator`, `emc_chamber`, `esd_simulator`, `shaker`, `climatic_chamber` | facility tokens: a missing one means "not on this bench" | no |

Numeric bounds (source maximum voltage, current and power, the approved
DUT-input overvoltage guard) come from the profile through `envelope()`.

## What runs here, and the numbers a recipe needs

`recipe_parameters()` derives these from the standard and the DUT rating
(12T12-4A, 9-36 V). None of these recipes exists yet as an executable
procedure; the general real recipe runner is still disabled and the
below-minimum steps need the approved UVLO-style recipe path (brief §7.5).

| Clause | 12 V | 24 V |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.2 | code C: 14 V -> 9 V -> 14 V -> 16 V -> 14 V; t1 30 s, t2 60 s; 1 V/s as 1 V live steps; mode 3.2 at room temperature | code E: 28 V -> 10 V -> 28 V -> 32 V -> 28 V; same timing; 32 V is above the 26 V guard |
| 4.3.1.1 | 18 V for 3600 s at room temperature (deviation recorded) | none: 36 V equals the DUT ceiling |
| 4.5 | 14 V -> 0 V -> 14 V at 0.5 V/min as 20 mV steps every 2.4 s; 700 steps and 1680 s per direction | 28 V -> 0 V -> 28 V; 1400 steps and 3360 s per direction |
| 4.6.1.2 (partial) | base 12 V; interruptions 1.0-2.0 s in 0.1 s steps (case 1) and a 1 s interruption with 1-10 s recoveries (case 2) by switching the source output off; the 10 us-1 s range is not covered | base 24 V; same |
| 4.6.2 | code C: 9 V then 8.55, 8.1, ... 0.45, 0 V (20 lows) each >= 5 s, back to 9 V for >= 10 s with a functional test; all lows are below the DUT minimum | code E: 10 V then 9.5, 9.0, ... 0 V; 18 lows are below the DUT minimum |

The "rated load" operating mode 3.4 is never reachable from a 1 A source for
this 48 W DUT; every verdict above carries that condition and the planner's
bounded load grid applies (brief §3.2). Ordinary supply/load polling is never
labelled as a ripple, transient, inrush or load-step measurement (brief §2).
